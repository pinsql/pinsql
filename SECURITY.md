# security policy

found something broken in one of my repos? nice. tell me before you tell the internet.

- **how:** DM [@wildkenyan](https://x.com/wildkenyan) or open a
  [private security advisory](https://github.com/pinsql/pinsql/security/advisories/new) on the affected repo.
- **what to send:** affected repo + commit, repro steps, impact. a PoC is great, a working exploit against someone else's box is not.
- **what you get:** a reply within 72h, credit in the fix if you want it, and my respect.

only test what you own or are allowed to. no scope, no shells.
